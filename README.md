# Selected Academic Research Portfolio

This portfolio presents selected academic and research projects completed during my IB Diploma Programme and extracurricular interests. 
The works reflect my interests in computer science, machine learning, mathematical modeling, scientific computing, data analysis, and interdisciplinary problem-solving.

Projects include research in handwritten digit clustering, Fourier analysis of musical chords, physics-based experimental investigation, historical analysis, literary interpretation, and Theory of Knowledge reflection.

Several of these projects were presented at the Science Symposium organized by the Frankfurt International School Science and Technology Commission (FISSTC), a student-led research organization where I served as Head of the Research Institute and Leader.
